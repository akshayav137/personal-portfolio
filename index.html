I volunteered at a local public library and during my sophomore year I helped with library book management, shelving, and pulling holds.

September 2025 - Present

I'm a Pitt Pathfinder (College Tour Guide) for the University of Pittsburgh. I did extensive training throughout my first semester as a first-year at Pitt. Beyond being a paid job on campus, this role gives us the opportunity to showcase what makes our university such an amazing place to prospective students. The training and the job so far is one of the best experiences of my life. It pulled me out of my shell and motivated me to interact and connect with people all over the world. Every week I meet people from around the country and the globe and interact with students and their families, taking on the job of easing them into a new chapter that can be quite daunting as a high school student.

I learned an extensive variety of skills, the main one being public speaking. During training, we had to learn and memorize around 3 1/2 hours worth of material about the University Campus as well as the City of Pittsburgh. Added to this, we needed to include flow, personality, and quick thinking when learning how to talk to families. Aside from public speaking, I also learned time management, as training semester was like taking an added 2 credit class and had quite a time commitment, and problem solving, interpersonal skills, storytelling and leadership.

This semester, I was also on the hiring team, which takes interviews for the incoming hiring class. I also met a lot of students through this opportunity and it helped my confidence and decision-making.
